Rank #3 Anime of 2026

BLEACH: Thousand-Year Blood War – The Calamity

The explosive conclusion to BLEACH’s legendary saga.

Why This Arc Earned Rank #3

The Calamity arc elevates BLEACH to its most intense and emotional heights yet. Ichigo faces immense pressure as he discovers the truth about his past, his powers, and the role he’s destined to fulfill in the final conflict. The battles are grand, swift, and fierce, with animation that makes every Bankai reveal feel like a monumental event. Rukia and Renji have some of their finest scenes in the entire series, highlighting their growth since the beginning. Yhwach’s presence makes the entire arc feel unpredictable, and every clash with the Quincy forces heightens the stakes even more. Squad Zero, Uryu’s choices, and Ichigo’s ultimate stand all add layers of tension and emotion that keep the story moving at a rapid pace. With massive battles, powerful character moments, and nonstop excitement, The Calamity arc demonstrates why BLEACH’s return is one of the biggest anime events of 2026.
